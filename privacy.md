# Immersive MC Application Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## 1. Overview

This privacy policy explains how the Immersive MC Discord application handles information when displaying Minecraft activity through Discord Rich Presence.

## 2. Information and Usage

The application is intended to display Minecraft gameplay status, such as the current dimension, through Discord Rich Presence. Depending on the configuration, this may include your Minecraft username and gameplay activity.

## 3. Data Collection and Storage

The developer does not intentionally collect or maintain a separate database of users' personal information through this application.

The Minecraft mod and Discord may process information as necessary to provide Rich Presence functionality. Their own privacy practices and policies apply to their respective services.

## 4. Third-Party Services

This application relies on Discord and Minecraft. Information processed by those services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

## 5. Data Security

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Users should review the privacy practices of any third-party services they use.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application or its functionality changes.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application developer through the project's official contact channel.
